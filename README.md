# Just do it.

Hi, I'm a software engineer with 5 years of experience across web, mobile, and server.

## 🚀 What I do now

- **At work, I'm an IT Lead (IL).** I lead large-scale projects, keeping the team and the work moving in the same direction.
- **On my own, I'm a product engineer.** With AI as my teammate, I take products from idea to launch by myself: planning, design, development, and operations.

## 🧭 What I care about

- **Thinking from the user's side.** Before I build something, I try to see it the way the person using it will.
- **Cost efficiency.** I like building things that run well without wasting money, so they can keep running for a long time.

## 🌱 What I'm aiming for

I want to build something small that lasts.
Not something loud, but something people keep coming back to and using, day after day.

## 🛠 How I work

I tend to think things over for a long time.
But I always try to start building instead of staying stuck in planning.
That's why my motto is just three words.
